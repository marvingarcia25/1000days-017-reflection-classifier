How this works

The model runs on the server. This page sends your text to the app's /classify endpoint; the C# classifier — trained once at startup on 150 labelled reflections — does the maths and returns the verdict plus each word's lean.

Multinomial Naive Bayes. It counts how often each word appears in positive vs. negative training text, adds up the log-probabilities for your sentence, and picks the more likely class. Add-one smoothing handles words it has never seen; a word after not / never / didn't is tagged separately, so "not happy" isn't read as "happy". Each bar above is one word's log-likelihood ratio — the model's real reasoning, not a summary.
